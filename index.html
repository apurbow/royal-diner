Quick information about the menu, ordering, and reservations.

What is Japanese yoshoku?

Yoshoku is Japanese-style Western cooking. At ROYAL DINER, that means comforting favourites such as omurice, hamburg steak, curry, pasta, and gratin.

Can I add dishes to my cart from the Home page?

Yes. Use Add to Cart on a featured dish. Open the cart icon in the navigation to review items and adjust quantities before continuing to the Order page.

How do I place an order?

Open the Order page, choose delivery, pickup, or eat-in, enter the requested details, and review the order before confirming.

Can I reserve a table in advance?

Yes. Visit the Reservation page to select a date, time, and guest count, then review your booking details before confirming.

Where can I find my order or reservation details?

Open the Order page to view order history, or the Reservation page to review upcoming and previous reservations.

What are the opening hours?

Lunch: 11:00–15:00. Dinner: 17:00–21:00. Hours may change for holidays or special occasions, so check the latest information before visiting.
